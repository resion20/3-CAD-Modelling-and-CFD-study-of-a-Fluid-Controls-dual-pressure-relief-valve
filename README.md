# 3-CAD-Modelling-and-CFD-study-of-a-Fluid-Controls-dual-pressure-relief-valve

1) Scope & Objective

Modeled and analyzed a Fluid Controls 1L22 dual pressure relief valve to quantify pressure losses and discharge behavior for system sizing.

2) CAD Modeling (SolidWorks)

Reverse-engineered and parametrized the full assembly; split bodies to expose internal galleries.

Documented operation with mates, section views, and an exploded diagram.

3) CFD Setup (SolidWorks Flow Simulation)

Configured steady, incompressible RANS with mineral oil properties.

Applied pressure/flow BCs at inlet/outlet; captured throttling at the relief orifice.

Refined the mesh locally at the seat, edges, and vena contracta to resolve shear/separation.

4) Mesh-Independence Study

Varied local refinement levels; tracked the discharge coefficient (Cd) stability.

Identified an optimal refinement where Cd converged ≈ 0.79, balancing accuracy and runtime.

5) Results & Interpretation

Generated pressure/velocity fields, streamlines, and Δp–Q characteristics.

6) Assumptions & Limits

Assumed quasi-steady, isothermal flow; treated cavitation qualitatively.

Noted that transient opening and viscosity–temperature coupling were outside scope.
